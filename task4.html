Best Lessons

Part 1: The Premise

Vagabond is a manga by Takehiko Inoue, based on the life of the swordsman Miyamoto Musashi. It starts in Japan after the battle of Sekigahara, where a wild and angry young man called Shinmen Takezo is left for dead. With the help of a monk named Takuan, he slowly changes and takes the name Musashi. From there he wanders the country, fighting duels and chasing one goal: to become the strongest swordsman alive.

Part 2: Its Impact on Life

After reading Vagabond, you start to look at strength differently. Winning is not the real point, controlling yourself is. It teaches patience, honesty with yourself and respect for the people who shape you. It also shows how lonely and costly an obsession can be, so many readers end up thinking about their own habits, their pride and what they are really working towards.
